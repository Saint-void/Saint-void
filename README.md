## 🚁 Projects

### Vera Drone Delivery

**Vera** is a drone delivery and logistics platform designed to demonstrate how autonomous drones can be integrated with software to manage delivery missions.

The system combines:

* Mission management
* Drone telemetry
* Mapping
* Autonomous flight
* Delivery coordinates
* MAVLink communication
* ArduPilot simulation
* FastAPI backend services
* Real-time fleet monitoring

The long-term vision is to develop **Vera into a logistics technology platform for Nigeria**.

### 🤖 Vella AI

**Vella** is an AI chatbot platform designed for businesses.

Businesses can provide their knowledge through documents, URLs, and other sources and use that information to power an AI assistant that can interact with their customers.

**Vision:**

> Train Once. Answer Everywhere.

The platform is being developed around AI integration, knowledge bases, APIs, authentication, and business automation.
